Hoppy Blocks Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026

In short: Hoppy Blocks has no accounts, does not ask for your name, email or any other personal information, and keeps your game progress on your device. The only outside service in the app is Google AdMob, which shows ads. Every ad request is marked as directed at children, limited to G-rated content, and not personalized.

Who we are

Hoppy Blocks is a block puzzle game made by an independent developer, Narek Sargsyan ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play Hoppy Blocks on iOS or Android.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store or share any personal information. There is no sign-up, no chat, no social features and no way for players to share anything with other people.

Your scores, Adventure progress, the game in progress and your sound and vibration settings are saved only on your device. They never leave it, and we cannot see them. Deleting the app deletes this data.

We do not use any analytics, crash-reporting or tracking tools of our own.

Advertising

Hoppy Blocks is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. Banner ads appear outside the game board, full-screen ads appear only between games, and video ads appear only when the player chooses to watch one (for example, to keep playing after running out of space).

Because the game is made for players of all ages, including children, we set up AdMob so that:

  • every ad request is tagged as child-directed (under the U.S. Children's Online Privacy Protection Act, COPPA) and as coming from a user under the age of consent (under the EU General Data Protection Regulation, GDPR);
  • only ads rated G (suitable for all audiences) are shown;
  • ads are never personalized, and there is no remarketing or interest-based advertising;
  • the app does not request the advertising identifier: on iOS it never shows the tracking prompt or reads the IDFA, and on Android it does not have the advertising ID permission.

To deliver ads, AdMob may still process limited technical information, such as your IP address (used to estimate a general location, such as the country), the type of device and operating system, app version, and how ads are viewed and tapped. Google uses this to show ads, limit how often the same ad appears, measure ad performance and prevent fraud. For child-directed requests, Google limits this processing as described in its policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services
  • AdMob and child-directed treatment

Children's privacy

Hoppy Blocks is designed to be safe for children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your country). The app has no in-app purchases, no outside links except those inside ads, and no way to contact other players. If you are a parent or guardian and believe information about your child has been collected, contact us and we will help.

Permissions

Hoppy Blocks does not ask for access to your camera, microphone, contacts, photos or precise location. It uses internet access only to load ads, and vibration only for haptic feedback, which you can turn off on the home screen.

Your choices

  • You can play offline. Without a connection, no ads load and nothing is sent anywhere.
  • You can delete all game data at any time by uninstalling the app.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal data. Since we hold none, requests about ad data are handled by Google through the links above, and we are happy to help you with them.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change reaches the app.
